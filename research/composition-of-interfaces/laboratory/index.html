How to read the construction

The frame fits the space available on your screen. Both outer margins come out before dividing the measure. Each column receives an equal share of what remains after the gutters. Margin and gutter limits follow the available width so that columns retain a positive measure. A span includes its internal gutters. The space after the title ends at the inside edge of the far margin and includes the next gutter, if there is one.

The horizontal rules mark baseline intervals. Four intervals make one module and one interval separates adjacent modules. The sheet has six modules and a single baseline interval outside them at the top and bottom. The drawn grid positions the blocks. The type inside each block has its own line height.
